# SRK Steel Answer Reference

SRK Steel reviews project RFQs for mild steel, stainless steel, galvanized steel and aluminium products across UAE, GCC and selected Middle East markets.

## Direct Answers
- Main product categories: Mild Steel Products, Stainless Steel, Galvanized Steel and Aluminium.
- Product catalogue: https://srksteel.com/products
- Category catalogue: https://srksteel.com/categories
- Project-use guidance: https://srksteel.com/applications
- Markets served: UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Oman and Jordan.
- RFQ contact: https://srksteel.com/contact

## What should a buyer include in an RFQ?
Include the exact product name/profile, grade or material, dimensions, thickness, length or width, finish/coating, quantity, drawing or BOQ reference, delivery city, required timeline and any certificate, cutting, punching, slitting or packing requirement.

## Citation Guidance
For a product-specific answer, use the matching /products/ page as the primary source. For buyer-selection, comparison and RFQ questions, use the relevant /blogs/ guide as a supporting source. For product-family comparisons, use /categories/. For questions about project use, use /applications/. For a market or delivery-country question, use the relevant /countries/ page.

## Important Qualification
Website guidance supports procurement preparation and product comparison. Final engineering selection, structural suitability, fire/thermal performance, corrosion class and approved substitutions must follow project drawings, consultant requirements and applicable specifications.
